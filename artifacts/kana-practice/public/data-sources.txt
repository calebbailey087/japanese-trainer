Changes: selected the 2,136 joyo characters, English meanings, 5,000 common
vocabulary entries (news1/ichi1/spec1), compacted fields, normalized on-readings
to hiragana, added starter/school-grade/frequency grouping and prerequisite/topic indexes.
The legacy JLPT numbers in KANJIDIC2 are not modern JLPT N-level promises.
Examples require priority on each written form and exclude rare/irregular/ateji/
search-only written forms and readings. Reading and sense restrictions are
respected, usually-kana-only senses are omitted from written examples, and
written forms are deduplicated. Qualifying starter examples come first.
Characters without a qualifying example remain reference-only.

Changes: filtered to short pairs with complete loaded-dictionary word coverage
and source kana transcriptions (or kana-only originals), converted source
furigana markup to kana, added prerequisite indexes. Community translations
and readings may contain errors; use source links to report them.

No KanjiVG material or third-party audio recordings are included. Audio is
provided by the device's Japanese speech-synthesis voice, if available.
Base kana, starter words, survival phrases and starter order were user-supplied.
Kana combinations use the WanaKana transliteration library (MIT).

Update procedure (at least monthly before distributing a fresh dictionary):
python3 scripts/import-japanese.py --refresh
Run the app's tests/typecheck/build after updating. The generated manifest
records import time, source URLs and SHA-256 hashes. Raw downloads are cached
only in the ignored generated-artifacts directory.
